FIELD NOTES

Make the reasoning visible.

Inside the Argument is an independent educational prototype. Its three original passages explore cultural institutions, urban evaluation, and translation. Every question and explanation is authored for this experience.

Built around CARS skills

The questions address foundations of comprehension, reasoning within the text, and reasoning beyond the text. Interactive evidence tracing, answer repair, and argument maps are learning activities, not features of the actual MCAT.

Read the AAMC’s official CARS overview ↗

A few useful distinctions

  • Evidence sets are selected, compact routes to the explanation. Other relevant sentences can also contribute.
  • Argument maps are instructional interpretations, not the only possible representations of a passage. Assumption cards state inferred commitments.
  • New findings in “What if?” are hypothetical. Assess their effect on the exact displayed claim.
  • Completion marks track activities explored, not validated mastery or an MCAT score.

Your progress stays here

Answers and exercise progress are saved in this browser only. There are no accounts, analytics, AI requests, or external asset requests. “Start over” resets the current passage.

 YOUR WAY INTO THE ARGUMENT

Read once.
Explore five ways.

  1. DecideRead the passage and commit to an answer. Then inspect every choice’s explanation.
  2. TraceSelect sentences in the passage and compare your evidence with a compact model route.
  3. RepairChange a flawed statement. Find the small edit that fixes a large reasoning error.
  4. What if?Predict what new evidence does to a precise claim, then reveal the connection.
  5. MapPlace cards into an argument. Drag, or select a card and then a slot.

There is no time limit. Revisit a passage, try another answer, or move freely between activities.
